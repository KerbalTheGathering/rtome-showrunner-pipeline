## When this is required

Use this agreement where contributions are made **by employees in the course of
their employment**, or where a company otherwise owns the intellectual property
in the work being contributed.

In most jurisdictions, code written by an employee within the scope of their
employment belongs to the employer, not the employee. Where that is so, the
individual cannot grant the rights in [CLA.md](CLA.md) — only the employer can.
An individual signature in that situation is worth nothing, which is exactly the
defect this document exists to prevent.

If you are contributing purely in a personal capacity, on your own equipment and
your own time, and your employment contract does not reach the work, you need
only [CLA.md](CLA.md).

## Terms

The terms of [CLA.md](CLA.md) §§1–12 are incorporated into this agreement in
full, with the following modifications:

### A. Substitution of the contracting party

**"You"** means the legal entity identified in the signature block below, and
all other entities that control, are controlled by, or are under common control
with that entity. For this purpose, "control" means (i) the power, direct or
indirect, to direct the management of such entity, whether by contract or
otherwise, or (ii) ownership of fifty per cent (50%) or more of the outstanding
shares, or (iii) beneficial ownership of such entity.

**"Contribution"** means a work of authorship submitted to the Licensor by any
Designated Employee, in the manner described in [CLA.md](CLA.md) §1.

### B. Authority

The individual executing this agreement represents that they are authorised to
bind the entity to it, and that the entity holds, or has secured, all rights
necessary to grant the licences in [CLA.md](CLA.md) §§2–4 over every
Contribution submitted by a Designated Employee.

### C. Designated employees

You shall maintain with the Licensor a schedule of the employees authorised to
submit Contributions on Your behalf, identified by GitHub account. You may add
to or remove from that schedule at any time by written notice to the Licensor.

Contributions submitted by an employee on the schedule are made under this
agreement. Contributions submitted by an employee **not** on the schedule are
not covered by it, and the Licensor may decline them or require an individual
agreement.

Removing an employee from the schedule takes effect prospectively only. It does
not revoke the licences already granted over Contributions previously
submitted, which are irrevocable under [CLA.md](CLA.md) §§2–4.

### D. Scope

This agreement covers Contributions submitted before as well as after its
execution date, to the extent You held the rights in them.
