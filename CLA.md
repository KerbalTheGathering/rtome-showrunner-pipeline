## Why this exists

This project is dual-licensed. The public licence is the GNU AGPL v3.0 with the
Licensor's Conditions; commercial users buy a separate licence from the Licensor. That
second half only works if the Licensor holds sufficient rights over **all** of
the code — including yours.

Without this agreement, a single merged patch under bare AGPL would make it
permanently impossible to grant a commercial licence covering that patch. This
document fixes that, and does so without taking your copyright away from you.

### In plain English

- **You keep your copyright.** This is a licence, not an assignment. Your
  contribution remains yours, and you may use it anywhere else, for anything,
  including in your own commercial work.
- **You let the Licensor sell it.** You grant the right to include your
  contribution in commercially licensed builds of this project.
- **You promise it is yours to give.** Not copied from somewhere else, and not
  owned by your employer.
- **The project stays open.** Under §6, your contribution will always remain
  available under a public source-available licence. It cannot be taken
  closed-only.

The plain-English summary is a convenience. The terms below govern.

## How to sign

Open a pull request and leave a comment containing exactly:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

The signature is recorded automatically against your GitHub account, with the
commit SHA and timestamp, in `.github/signatures/cla.json`. You sign once; the
agreement covers all your future Contributions to the Project unless you notify
the Licensor otherwise in writing.

If you are contributing in the course of employment, your employer must also
execute [CLA-ENTITY.md](CLA-ENTITY.md).
