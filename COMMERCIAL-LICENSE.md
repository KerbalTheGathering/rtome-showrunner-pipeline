The default licence — [LICENSE](LICENSE) — is the **GNU AGPL v3.0 with three
Conditions**: no selling the Software (the Commons Clause), no use by large
organizations, and no synthesizing a real person's likeness without their
written consent. It is free and copyleft for the people it is meant for. A
separate commercial licence lifts the first two conditions and the AGPL's
copyleft obligations. Nothing lifts the third.

## Which one do I need?

**You do not need a commercial licence if you are** a natural person, a
registered nonprofit, or an organization whose total gross revenue plus
funding was **under US $1,000,000** in its last fiscal year. Within that, you
may:

- run the pipeline and make films with it, including films you sell
- modify it, fork it, publish your fork
- teach with it, write about it, study it
- run it on a server for yourself or your collaborators

The **output** of the pipeline — your films, your frames, your audio — is
yours. This licence covers the pipeline, not what you make with it.

**You do need a commercial licence if:**

- you are, or you are using it for or on behalf of, an organization above
  the US $1,000,000 threshold — **including purely internal use**. An
  employer directing employees to use this pipeline is the case this
  condition exists for
- you sell, resell, or sublicense the Software itself
- you offer it as a hosted or managed service (SaaS), paid or ad-supported
- you charge for consulting, support, or integration whose value derives
  substantially from this Software
- you bundle it into a paid product or service, or ship it inside a
  closed-source product

**No licence, at any price, permits** using the pipeline to synthesize an
identifiable real person's likeness or voice without that person's freely
given written consent. Consent extracted as a condition of employment does
not count. This term is not for sale.

If you are unsure which side of a line you are on, ask. The short version:
**individuals and small teams create freely; large organizations pay;
nobody fakes a real person.**

## What a commercial licence gives you

- a royalty-free, non-exclusive right to Sell the Software as defined in the
  Commons Clause
- release from the Organizational Use condition, so your organization may
  use the pipeline at whatever scale is agreed
- release from the AGPL's copyleft and network-source-disclosure obligations
  (§5 and §13), so you may keep your modifications and your surrounding
  product closed
- terms negotiated to the deployment you actually have

It does **not** and will not include release from the Likeness Consent
condition.

Pricing depends on scale and use. Small shops and solo operators are not
charged the same as platforms.

## How to obtain one

Email **licensing@rtome.net** with:

1. who you are — individual, company, or project
2. what you want to build or ship
3. how it is deployed — internal, distributed binary, hosted service
4. rough scale — seats, users, or installations

You will get a quote and a licence agreement. Nothing here is a contract; the
executed agreement is.
